How I work

The habits behind the crash-free number.

Own the whole release

As the only Android engineer on ExpiWell, I'm responsible from architecture decision to Play Store rollout to watching Crashlytics afterwards. Nothing gets handed off, so nothing gets dropped.

Design for the worst network

Research participants fill in surveys on trains, in basements, on prepaid data. Room plus WorkManager means every response is saved first and synced when it can be, and a local plus push notification pipeline keeps delivery reliable.

Async across time zones

Years of working with stakeholders in the US, EU and APAC. Clear written updates, decisions documented in the ticket, and 3–5 features shipped per sprint without needing overlapping hours.

Ship with a safety net

JUnit and Mockito for logic, Espresso for the screens people touch, and GitHub Actions with Fastlane so a release is a pipeline, not a checklist. Code reviews double as mentoring for junior developers.
